# Global Superstore Business Intelligence Report

## 1. Executive Summary

This project uses Power BI to analyze the Global Superstore dataset and understand the company's sales, profit, customers, products, and regional performance. The dashboard was designed to make important business information easier to understand and compare. The company generated around $12.64M in sales and $1.47M in profit. One of the main findings is that the Consumer segment contributes more than half of total sales, making it an important area for the business to focus on.

## 2. Business Problem

The main purpose of this dashboard is to understand how the business is performing and identify areas that need attention. It helps answer questions such as which customer segments and product categories perform best, how sales change over time, and where the business can improve profitability. The dashboard gives management a simple way to compare these areas and make better decisions.

## 3. Data & Methodology

The analysis uses the Global Superstore dataset, which contains information about orders, customers, products, regions, sales, and profit. The data was checked for quality, including missing values and duplicate records, before being used for analysis. The original data was then organized into separate tables for sales and descriptive information such as customers, products, regions, and dates. Power BI was used to create the dashboard and calculate the main business measures.

## 4. Key Insights

### 1. Strong Overall Performance

The company generated around $12.64M in sales and $1.47M in profit, showing good overall performance. The business should continue growing sales while making sure that profit remains strong.

### 2. Consumer Segment Leads Sales

The Consumer segment generated around $6.5M, or 51.48% of total sales, making it the biggest contributor. The company should focus on retaining these customers and encouraging repeat purchases.

### 3. Technology Is the Strongest Category

Technology has the highest sales and around a 14% profit margin, making it one of the strongest categories. The company should continue focusing on this category and understand what is helping it perform well.

### 4. Furniture Has Lower Profitability

Furniture has a profit margin of around 7%, which is lower than Technology and Office Supplies. The company should review Furniture pricing and costs to find ways to improve its profitability.

### 5. Sales Change During the Year

Sales changed throughout the year, with November and December being stronger months and February being one of the weaker months. The company could prepare inventory and marketing campaigns for stronger periods and use promotions to improve weaker months.

## 5. Limitations

This analysis is based only on the information available in the Global Superstore dataset. It does not include factors such as marketing spending, customer satisfaction, competitor activity, or current market conditions. The dashboard also describes historical data, so the trends should not automatically be treated as predictions of future performance.

## 6. Recommended Next Steps

The company should focus on maintaining strong performance in the Consumer and Technology areas while looking for ways to improve Furniture profitability. It should also investigate the reasons behind weaker sales months and plan promotions or inventory accordingly. In the future, adding information such as marketing costs, customer feedback, and more recent data could provide a deeper understanding of business performance.
